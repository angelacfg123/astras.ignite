Mars

InSight Lander

Description: InSight, short for Interior Exploration using Seismic Investigations, Geodesy and Heat Transport, was a Mars lander designed to give the Red Planet its first thorough checkup since it formed 4 billion years ago. The lander was built on the proven design of NASA's Mars Phoenix lander. InSight's robotic arm was over 5 feet 9 inches (1.8 meters) long. It lifted a seismometer and heat flow probe from the deck and placed them on the surface.

Phoenix Lander

Description: Phoenix Lander was a stationary robotic spacecraft designed to study the geology, ice, and atmospheric conditions of the Martian Arctic. It was built using a modified, pre-existing airframe from the canceled Mars Surveyor 2001 Lander, alongside instruments originally developed for the 2001 mission and the ill-fated Mars Polar Lander

Moon

Lunar Module Spacecraft

Description: The Apollo Lunar Module (LM) was the two-stage spacecraft designed to ferry astronauts between lunar orbit and the surface of the Moon during NASA's Apollo program

Apollo Lunar Descent Shell

Description: The Apollo Lunar Module descent stage served as the platform that landed the crew on the Moon and acted as the launch pad for the ascent stage. The outer "shell" was wrapped in multiple layers of aluminized Mylar and Kapton foil thermal blankets, protecting the interior fuel tanks, hypergolic propellants, and equipment quadrants from extreme solar radiation and lunar temperature swings
