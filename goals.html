Project Details

Project ASTRAS IGNITE focuses on retelling the stories of abandoned equipment on the Moon and Mars using a highly interactive and accessible website as a free learning tool to educate people from around the world about the impacts of these equipment, and how they changed the history of space. By making this project, we hope that the people can increase their knowledge about the Moon and Mars space programs, build a deep emotional bridge between the public and space exploration, and change their perspective on why many landers and rovers have been discarded. We believe that ASTRAS IGNITE can benefit space agencies, science education, and global heritage. Beyond inspiring awe, these narratives directly support funding, engineering progress, and space policy. To bring this project to life, we used Visual Studio code for the main base, and added the final, creative details using Canva, Ibispaint, and Procreate. Using these applications, we were able to make drawings of the desired space equipment designs, create our official project logo, and make website assets (digital menu, header). Unlike any other projects, we added responsive hover effects, custom cursor states, and light motion (GIFs) that reward exploration without lagging load times. We also added intentional visuals and depth by using hand-drawn illustrations, and physical textures scanned into digital form.
